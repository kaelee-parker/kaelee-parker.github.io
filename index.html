My CV

I am an observational extragalactic astronomer specializing in the multi-wavelength study of ionizing-photon escape, stellar feedback, and chemical enrichment in low-metallicity star-forming galaxies. My work combines rest-frame UV through mid-infrared spectroscopy from HST, JWST, and ground-based facilities to build empirical frameworks connecting the structure of the interstellar medium to the ionizing output of massive stars. I have extensive experience with spectral modeling, photoionization analysis, IFU data reduction, and statistical comparisons between galaxies in the nearby universe and at high-redshifts. My research bridges nearby resolved systems with the distant galaxies revealed by JWST, with a particular focus on how feedback and gas geometry regulate the escape of ionizing radiation across cosmic time.

Research Keywords: UV/optical/radio/IR spectroscopy • ISM/CGM physics • Ionizing photon escape • Stellar feedback • Low-metallicity galaxies • JWST + HST observations • IFU analysis • Photoionization modeling • Galaxy evolution and reionization
