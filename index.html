I am an observational extragalactic astronomer specializing in the multi-wavelength study of ionizing-photon escape, stellar feedback, and chemical enrichment in low-metallicity star-forming galaxies. My work combines rest-frame UV through mid-infrared spectroscopy with HST/COS and JWST/MIRI to constrain covering fractions, ionization structure, temperature/density variations, and direct-method metallicities.

I’m a member of the COS Legacy Spectroscopic SurveY (CLASSY) and I develop analysis tools—including a MIRI MRS PSF-mitigation pipeline for extended mapping—to link local, low-metallicity galaxies to conditions in the early universe. Current projects include spatially resolved emission-line mapping, LIS vs. H I covering fraction comparisons, and predictors of ionizing escape fraction relevant to reionization-era analogs.
