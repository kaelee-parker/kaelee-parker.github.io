Teaching Philosophy

I design learning experiences that foreground scientific reasoning, reproducibility, and student agency. In astronomy, coding and data analysis are gateways to discovery—so I scaffold assignments that move from guided notebooks to open-ended projects, emphasize clear communication, and normalize iteration and peer feedback.

Mentoring Commitments
  • Promote transparent, version-controlled workflows that students can carry into future research.
  • Establish clear milestones, frequent check-ins, and written takeaways after each meeting.
  • Support inclusive practices: accessible materials, flexible participation, and acknowledgment of diverse paths into astronomy.
