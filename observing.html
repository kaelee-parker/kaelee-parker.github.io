Observing Programs

HST and JWST programs focused on ISM structure and ionizing-photon escape

JWST/MIRI MRS — Low-Z ISM Maps
  • Goal: map ionization structure and hardness via [Ne III]/[Ne II], [S IV]/[Ne II].
  • Targets: six nearby, low-metallicity galaxies spanning ionization levels.
  • Deliverables: PSF-mitigated emission-line maps; public pipeline and example notebooks.
HST/COS — CLASSY & LzLCS+
  • Goal: LIS vs. H I covering fractions and their relation to \( f_{\mathrm{esc}} \).
  • Products: uniform measurements, comparisons across samples, public data tables.
IFU Mapping of Lensed Galaxies
  • Goal: resolve \( T_e \), \( n_e \), and Z on 200–500 pc scales to test mixing vs. localized enrichment.
  • Status: revised proposals building on prior cycles and referee feedback.
Notes

I actively develop proposal revisions (e.g., HST Cycle 33; JWST Cycle 5) incorporating new diagnostics, improved figures, and focused samples.
