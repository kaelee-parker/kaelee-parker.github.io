Research Overview

connecting local star-forming galaxies to the physics of reionization

ORCID
NASA/ADS Library
My CV
Themes
  • Lyman-continuum escape: empirical predictors from LIS and H I covering fractions, β-slope, O32, Lyα velocity structure.
  • Mid-IR ionization diagnostics: spatially resolved MIRI/MRS emission-line and ratio maps to trace ionization, hardness, and shocks.
  • Direct-method abundances: \( T_e \), \( n_e \), and metallicity gradients; turbulence vs. localized enrichment.
  • Tools & pipelines: forward-modeled PSF removal for MIRI/MRS extended sources (in progress).
Selected Projects
CLASSY & LzLCS+

Unified analysis of LIS vs. H I covering fractions and their relation to \( f_{\mathrm{esc}} \).

JWST/MIRI emission-line maps

[Ne III]/[Ne II], [S IV]/[Ne II], H I recombination in six nearby metal-poor galaxies; PSF-mitigated maps.

Direct-method gradients at high-z

Leveraging lensing and IFU data to resolve T$_e$, n_e, and metallicity on 200–500 pc scales.
